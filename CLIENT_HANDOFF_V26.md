# Client police tactics and neighborhood crews — v0.26

Branch codex/living-city-v26, stacked on codex/city-ai-v25. Reserved client scope through Claude PR #3. Reviewed server v0.9 handoff and 31305d3: Claude owns online/server/shared/worker/workflows and claude/online-play, which integrates client branches separately. No files in those areas changed; do not merge the client stack automatically.

Police choose shelter on the far side of nearby stationary cars, route there, alternate holding and clear side peeks, and fire from the peek only after arriving and confirming an unobstructed line. Moving cover is discarded, losing sight returns to last-known investigation/search, and a second unit can approach a clear flank if no suitable shelter exists. Sightings are radioed to other units; hidden movement is still not followed. Reports are throttled and small memory updates avoid rerouting every simulation step. Reinforcements retain dispatch delays and now cap at four officers. Cars remain projectile/movement cover, not sight occluders. Officer idle/walking animation follows actual movement. Searches use staggered street waypoints, not enterable interior searches.

New local crew-reputation foundation: Southside Union and Harbor Crew. Territory follows Southside/Docks district boundaries. Courier delivery into a territory earns +8 trust there and −2 with the rival; robbery costs 12 and each actual civilian bullet hit costs 8 locally. Reputation clamps to −100..100; −20 is hostile and +20 trusted. Hostile crews refuse local dealer buy/sell actions; neutral contacts offer existing courier work/GPS; hostile contacts accept a $250 offering for +10 trust; trusted contacts give one carried medkit with a persisted three-minute refill. Capacity, proximity, life, vehicle, heat, line of sight and modal guards apply. Supplies follow existing pocket/death custody. Crew contacts use contextual USE, appear in MAP, and both reputations appear in STATS; entering hostile territory warns once on transition. No new permanent action button or modal. Contacts are noncombat service NPCs.

Crew progress is saved under nightshift-city-crews-v1 and joins backup restore/undo. Missing/invalid data migrate to bounded neutral state. This is client-local gameplay, not secure online economy, gang combat, captured territory, faction wars, random street events or new enterable buildings. Those remain future increments rather than claimed features.
